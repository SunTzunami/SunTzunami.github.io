Insho 印章

Hands-Free, Vision-Controlled PDF Reader for Distance & Mess-Free Reading

Computer Vision HCI Google MediaPipe WebAssembly React 19 TypeScript

Insho 印章 is an intelligent, camera-powered PDF reader that lets you navigate, scroll, and zoom documents completely hands-free using natural hand gestures. No mice, no keyboards, and no touchscreen smudges: designed specifically for reading comfortably from across your desk or while having meals.

“As a kid, I used to read books while having meals. Now I read technical papers, textbooks, and ebooks on my computer, which can get messy if I keep them close while eating. I wanted a way to read from a comfortable distance away from my screen without constantly wiping my hands to touch the keyboard or trackpad.”

Insho 印章: hands-free document navigation and gesture control while dining.

Key Features

  • Touchless Navigation: Turn pages forward or backward, scroll in any direction, and zoom smoothly without physical contact.
  • Floating Virtual Joystick: An intuitive open-palm joystick mode that lets you scroll vertically or horizontally based on relative hand displacement.
  • Smart Axis Locking: Automatically detects whether you intend to scroll vertically or horizontally and locks the axis to eliminate jitter and diagonal drift.
  • Proportional Pinch-to-Zoom: Pinch thumb and index finger to scale the document between 50% and 400% zoom with fine-grained control.
  • Instant Zoom Reset: Quickly snap back to default 100% view using a simple "OK" hand sign.
  • Pause & Resume Tracking: Toggle gesture recognition on or off with the Spiderman sign so you can eat, take notes, or talk without accidentally triggering gestures.
  • 100% Private & Local: All computer vision inference runs strictly on-device in WebAssembly/WebGL. No video, images, or documents are ever uploaded to the cloud.

Gesture Controls & How It Works

Gesture Hand Pose Action Mechanism & Parameters
Virtual Joystick Open Palm (all 5 fingers extended) Smooth 2D Scroll (Up / Down / Left / Right) Anchors initial hand position; displacement scales speed with ±12% deadzone and axis locking.
Next Page Two Fingers Pointing Down (Index + Middle down) Next Page Flips to the subsequent page with a 1000ms debounce cooldown.
Previous Page Two Fingers Pointing Up (Index + Middle up) Previous Page Flips to the preceding page with a 1000ms debounce cooldown.
Pinch-to-Zoom Index + Thumb Pinch Dynamic Zoom (50% – 400%) Spreading index and thumb zooms in; bringing them closer zooms out.
Reset Zoom "OK" Sign (Thumb & index tips touching) Reset to 100% Restores page scaling to default 1.0x with a 1000ms cooldown.
Pause / Resume "Spiderman" Sign (Thumb, index & pinky extended) Toggle Tracking Freeze Freezes gesture recognition until toggled again with a 1500ms safety guard.

Under the Hood: Gesture Classification Logic

Insho 印章 translates raw landmark coordinates into semantic interactions through spatial geometric checks:

  1. Finger Extension Heuristic: A finger is considered extended if the Euclidean distance from the wrist (Landmark 0) to its fingertip exceeds the distance from the wrist to its PIP joint.
  2. Pointing Direction: Comparing fingertip Y-coordinates against metacarpophalangeal (MCP) joints determines upward versus downward orientation.
  3. Virtual Joystick Mechanics: Upon transitioning into Open Palm, the initial wrist coordinate is recorded as the neutral anchor point. Continuous displacement offsets are computed each frame with an active deadzone of 12% to prevent drift. Once the threshold is broken, the axis locks to whichever direction has greater magnitude until reset.
  4. Continuous Pinch Math: Isolates the Euclidean distance between Landmark 4 (thumb tip) and Landmark 8 (index tip), applying deadband filtering to prevent jitter on release.
